Most meal planners assume you have already decided what to spend. Lazeez starts
from monthly income instead and works down: income divided by the days in the
current month gives a daily budget, split 20/60/20 across breakfast, lunch and
dinner. Nutrition runs on a parallel track, deriving BMI and a daily calorie
target from height, weight, gender and activity level, then splitting that
25/40/35 across the same three meals.

A recipe only reaches the customer if it clears both limits, so suggestions stay
inside the budget and the calorie target at the same time.

## Architecture

Three Express services over one MongoDB database. The Market module owns
supermarkets, sellers, ingredients, recipes and orders. The Financial module
computes budgets. The Meal module handles search and matching.

The two calculations are independent and slow enough not to belong in a request
cycle, so the services talk over RabbitMQ rather than calling each other
directly: the budget and calorie figures for each meal are published to their
own queues, and the Meal module waits on both before it answers. Users are one
Mongoose model with discriminators for customer, seller and admin, and routes
are guarded by JWT middleware.
