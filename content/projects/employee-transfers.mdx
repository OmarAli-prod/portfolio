A yearly system that moves thousands of employees between government
directorates. I built the frontend, covering HR validation, automated scoring,
managerial approvals, and administrative auditing against a secure relational
database.

It also embeds an OCR module that verifies submitted documents at over 95%
correctness, using OpenCV preprocessing and EasyOCR for Arabic, with fuzzy
matching to handle inconsistent document formatting.
