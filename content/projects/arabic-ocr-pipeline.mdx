The pipeline takes scanned handwritten Arabic ownership documents from image
preprocessing in OpenCV through to a structured DOCX output per document.

Handwritten Arabic is the hardest failure mode in OCR, and ligatures are why:
letters change shape depending on what sits beside them, so character
segmentation tuned for Latin script does not transfer. Reaching 99% accuracy per
document meant tuning the preprocessing and the OCR ensemble together rather
than separately, with most of that attention going to ligatures.

Batch processing dropped from five hours to under ten minutes.
