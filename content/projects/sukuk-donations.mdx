A sukuk is an Islamic bond. This platform covers the full lifecycle of one:
CRM, ERP, inventory, and distribution across multiple endowment units. It is
live in production and has generated over 100M EGP in profit to date.

The hard part was the data model. Donor management, endowment unit accounting,
physical asset inventory, and disbursement workflows all have to stay consistent
with each other, while respecting Islamic finance compliance rules around fund
segregation. Those rules shaped the schema rather than sitting on top of it as
validation.
