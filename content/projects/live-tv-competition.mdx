Dawry Al-A'imma Al-Nujaba is a live televised competition. I owned the
frontend end to end: real-time answers and scoring across multiple touchscreens,
synced via webhooks.

Several physical touchscreens have to agree on the current question and score
while the show is airing, with no opportunity to pause and reconcile. It has
aired across two events so far, with strong audience feedback.
