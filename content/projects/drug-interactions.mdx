A clinical decision engine covering more than 10,000 drug combinations, built
for live clinical safety workflows. An ETL pipeline pulls FDA data sources into
SQL Server, and a React dashboard handles filtering.

A join across combination tables is the obvious approach, but its response time
degrades as the combination count grows. Modeling the interactions as a graph
and traversing it holds responses under 200ms at scale.
